What does Globe Skillz do?

Globe Skillz is a Shopify SEO studio with development built in. I help Shopify stores rank higher on Google and in AI search, and I make the theme changes that SEO needs myself.

Do you work with stores in my country?

Yes. I work with Shopify merchants worldwide: the US, UK, EU, Middle East, Australia, Pakistan and elsewhere. Work is remote, with an agreed overlap window for your timezone.

How much does Shopify SEO cost?

Monthly Shopify SEO starts at $500/mo. One-time audits start at $200 and store builds at $400. See the pricing page for details.

Why Shopify only?

Working on one platform means I know its quirks, like duplicate product paths, tag pages, Markets and app bloat, and can fix them quickly. Every client benefits from that.
