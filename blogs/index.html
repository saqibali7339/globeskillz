SEO tips that actually help.

Plain-English guides on SEO, WordPress, and Shopify. Written for business owners, not marketers. No jargon, no fluff.

What I write about.

The questions business owners actually ask me: what SEO costs, how to hire an SEO company without getting burned, how to get a WordPress site onto Google, what a Shopify SEO expert really does. Every guide answers one question properly, with the honest trade-offs included.

Who this is for.

Owners doing their own marketing, or deciding who to hire to do it for them. I write the way I talk to clients: plain English, no jargon, no fluff, and no pressure. If a post makes you want a second pair of eyes on your site, the free audit offer at the end of every article is the natural next step.

Start with the fundamentals.

New here? The cost and hiring guides are the best first read. They answer the two questions almost every owner asks before spending a dollar on SEO: what should this cost, and who should I trust with it. From there, follow whatever problem you are trying to solve.
