Poland's UODO has become one of Europe's most aggressive data protection authorities — 64.3 million zloty in fines in 2025 alone, including 27.1 million against Poczta Polska. Your cookie consent needs a real, equally prominent refuse option, and your analytics must respect it. Beyond the fine risk, bad consent breaks your measurement: if half your visitors refuse tracking through a non-compliant banner, your SEO reporting is fiction. I build consent setups that satisfy UODO expectations while keeping your data clean, because in Poland compliance and measurability are the same project.

With and without diacritics, the same Polish word is effectively two different keywords with different search volumes — and many Poles search without diacritics out of habit or keyboard convenience. On top of that, Polish inflection means cases change word endings, creating further variants. Proper Polish keyword research maps all of it: diacritic and non-diacritic forms, inflected variants, and the intent behind each. Agencies that research only the dictionary form are targeting a fraction of the real demand. I build the full variant map before writing a single page.
