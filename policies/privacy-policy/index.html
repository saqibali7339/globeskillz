Privacy Policy.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

I take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information I collect when you visit this website or contact me — and what I do with it. Short version: I collect only what I need to reply to you and do the work, and I never sell your data.

1. Information I collect

When you use the contact form, I receive your name, email address, the service you’re interested in, your budget range, and your message. If we talk by email, I keep that correspondence. My hosting provider also logs basic technical data (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site running securely.

2. How I use it

I use your information to respond to your inquiry, prepare quotes, deliver the services you hire me for, and improve this website. I don’t use it for anything else, and there’s no newsletter or marketing list to get added to.

3. Form processing

Contact form submissions are processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form service that delivers your message to my email inbox. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policy.

4. Sharing

I never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. I share it only where necessary to operate — for example with my hosting provider — or where required by law.

5. Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. There’s nothing to opt out of.

6. Data retention

I keep your information for as long as needed to handle your inquiry or our working relationship. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

7. Your rights

You can ask me what data I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email info@globeskillz.com and I’ll take care of it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I’ll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Questions?

If anything here is unclear, email me at info@globeskillz.com — I answer everything personally.
