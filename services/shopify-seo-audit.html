What makes this audit different

Automated tools are good at finding symptoms and bad at telling you what matters. A tool will flag 4,000 “duplicate titles” without noticing that they all come from one tag-filter template, which is a single fix in Liquid.

I group findings by root cause, so 4,000 warnings turn into three tasks, each with a clear before and after.

What you receive

  • A written audit, organized by priority: fix now, fix this quarter, nice to have
  • A screen-recorded walkthrough of the biggest findings, so your team understands the reasons behind each fix
  • Code snippets or exact theme locations for every technical fix
  • A keyword-to-page map for your top collections
  • A 30-minute call to go through questions

Algorithm-hit stores

If your traffic dropped after a core, spam or helpful-content update, the audit includes a look at quality: thin or near-duplicate pages, doorway-style location or keyword pages, and content that doesn’t show first-hand expertise. These are the patterns updates penalize, and fixing them is usually the first step to recovering.

FAQ

Shopify SEO Audit questions

Have a different question? Ask me on WhatsApp.

How much does a Shopify SEO audit cost?

Audits start at $200 for stores up to roughly 1,000 URLs. Larger catalogs or multi-market setups are quoted after a quick look. If you start a monthly retainer within 30 days, the audit fee is credited back.

Will you implement the fixes?

If you want me to. Many clients hand the audit to their in-house developer. Others hire me to implement it as a fixed project or as part of a retainer.

What access do you need?

Shopify collaborator access (read-only is fine to start), Search Console and GA4. Ahrefs or Semrush access helps but isn’t required, since I use my own tools.

Can an audit help recover from a Google update?

It’s the right first step. The audit shows which pages Google is likely seeing as low-value, and that tells you what to merge, improve or remove.
