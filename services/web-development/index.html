Web Development

Most small-business websites are brochures nobody reads.

Here's what I see every week: a business owner paid good money for a website that takes six seconds to load, looks identical to their competitor's, and has a contact form nobody has ever filled in. Then they tell me ‘my website doesn't work.’ They're right — it doesn't, because it was never built to.

I build websites with one job: turn your visitors into enquiries. Every layout decision I make starts from the same question — what does your customer need to see to pick up the phone? Fast loading, clear messaging, and a big obvious next step. That's the whole philosophy, and it works.

The service

What I actually do for you.

01 / What I build

WordPress and Shopify. Nothing else.

I don't do React, Next.js, or custom web apps — and I'll tell you that upfront rather than learn on your money. WordPress and Shopify are what I know inside out, which means your site gets built fast, built right, and on platforms you can actually manage yourself afterwards. Business sites, e-commerce stores, landing pages, blogs — if it runs on WordPress or Shopify, I can build it.

02 / How I work

You talk to me. Only me.

No account managers, no juniors, no telephone game. You tell me about your business, I ask annoying questions about your customers, and then I design around the answers. You'll see the homepage first for feedback before I build a single inner page — so there are no surprise reveals at the end, only a site you've already approved piece by piece.

Deliverables

What you get with every build.

  • A site that loads in under 2 seconds — because every extra second costs you customers, and I optimize images, code, and caching from day one.
  • Mobile-first design — most of your customers will meet you on a phone. Your site will look deliberate there, not squeezed.
  • Copy guidance — I'm not a copywriter, but I'll tell you honestly when your text is confusing your customers, and help you fix it.
  • SEO foundations baked in — clean URLs, proper headings, meta tags, and sitemap, so Google can actually read your new site.
  • Easy editing — you'll be able to update text, photos, and products yourself. I'll record you a personal walkthrough video.
  • Launch support — I don't disappear after go-live. I stay around to fix the niggles and make sure everything holds up.
Process

How we'll work together.

STEP 1

Free teardown

You show me your current site (or your idea), and I tell you honestly what's wrong with it and what I'd do differently. Free, no pitch attached.

STEP 2

Blueprint

We agree on pages, structure, and the one action you want visitors to take. You approve the homepage design before anything else gets built.

STEP 3

Build

I build the full site on WordPress or Shopify — fast, responsive, and tested on real phones, not just my monitor.

STEP 4

Launch + handover

We go live together, I fix any launch-week niggles, and you get your walkthrough video so you're never dependent on me.
