Shopify SEO

Shopify makes selling easy. Ranking is the hard part.

Shopify handles the storefront beautifully — checkout, payments, inventory, all done. But Google decides who actually walks through the door, and Shopify ships with SEO handicaps: rigid URL structures, duplicate collection and product URLs, thin auto-generated copy, and app-bloated themes that load like it’s 2015. Most store owners never see these problems. They just see flat traffic and rising ad bills.

That’s where I come in. I fix the platform-specific issues holding your store back, rebuild your collections and product pages around the keywords buyers actually type, and strip the bloat slowing you down. The result: product pages that rank, collections that capture category searches, and a store that earns sales while you sleep — without paying Google for every click.

The service

What I actually fix and build.

01 / Technical Shopify SEO

Fix what the platform breaks.

Duplicate content from Shopify’s URL variants, wasted crawl budget, messy robots rules, missing canonicals, app scripts strangling your load time — I audit and fix the technical issues specific to Shopify. Your store gets a clean foundation Google can actually crawl and trust.

02 / Collections, products & content

Pages built to rank and convert.

I restructure your collections around real search demand, rewrite product and collection copy that sells to humans and ranks for Google, and build internal linking that pushes authority to the pages that make you money. Every money page earns its place on page one.

Deliverables

What’s covered in every Shopify SEO engagement.

  • Full Shopify technical audit — URL structure, duplicates, crawl budget, indexation, robots, sitemaps.
  • Collection and product page optimization — titles, copy, and metadata built around buying keywords.
  • Speed optimization — app audit, theme cleanup, image and code fixes for green Core Web Vitals.
  • Product and review schema — rich results with prices, ratings, and stock that win the click.
  • Site architecture and internal linking — authority flowing to the pages that generate revenue.
  • Content strategy — buying guides and category content that capture shoppers before they choose a competitor.
