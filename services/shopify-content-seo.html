How I approach Shopify Content SEO

Saqib Ali

Why most Shopify blogs don’t produce sales

The typical Shopify blog is a set of brand-news posts that nobody searches for, with no links to products. They have no target keyword and no clear purpose, so Google has little reason to rank them.

Content that works is planned from your collections outward. First I list the questions shoppers ask before buying from each collection. Then I write the single best answer to each question, with your products included where they actually help.

Built for Google and for AI answers

AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity pull from pages that answer clearly, define terms and back up their claims. Every piece is structured for this: a direct answer up top, scannable sections, comparison tables and a named author with real credentials.

The jewelry store in my results grew from a brand-new domain to 2.81M impressions in three months. Content clusters around its main collections did most of that work.

FAQ

Shopify Content SEO questions

Have a different question? Ask me on WhatsApp.

How many blog posts does a Shopify store need?

Fewer than most people think. Ten excellent, well-linked guides around your main collections usually beat a hundred thin posts. Quality and internal linking matter more than how often you publish.

Who writes the content?

I plan and edit everything. Writing is done by me or by experienced e-commerce writers I’ve worked with for years. Your team adds product expertise, and that first-hand input is what makes the content stand out.

Do I own the content?

Yes, completely. Everything is published to your Shopify blog under your brand.

Can content help with AI search results?

Yes. Clear, well-structured, factual content written by a named author is what AI systems tend to quote. See the AI search optimization service for more detail.
