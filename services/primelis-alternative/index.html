Primelis alternative: when a senior freelance SEO is the better fit

Primelis is a well-known French digital marketing agency. If you are comparing it with other options, you are probably weighing a big, multi-channel team against a smaller, more direct setup.

VERIFIED FACTS

What Primelis offers

Based on Primelis's own public materials and third-party company profiles (last checked September 2026 — confirm details on their website):

  • A French agency headquartered near Paris (Levallois-Perret), founded in 2009, with offices including Nantes, New York and Miami.
  • A team of 150+ people working with international clients.
  • A multi-channel offering: SEO alongside paid search, social ads and data services.
  • Proprietary software tools built in-house to support its SEO, paid and data work.
  • A positioning toward large accounts with substantial budgets across several markets.

That model is well suited to companies that need many disciplines coordinated under one roof.

HONEST GUIDANCE

Where a large agency is the right choice

Choose a firm like Primelis if:

  • You need SEO, paid media, social and data run together by one organization.
  • You operate in many countries or languages at once.
  • Your budget is large enough that a dedicated team and proprietary tooling are a reasonable spend.
  • Your company requires formal procurement, contracts and a team large enough to cover holidays and turnover.

If that describes you, a freelancer is not the right fit — and I'll tell you so.

THE OTHER SIDE

Where a freelance SEO specialist is the better fit

Choose an independent specialist if:

  • SEO is your main need, and you don't need a paid media or social team bundled in.
  • You are a small or mid-sized business, an ecommerce store, or a service company working in one main market or a few.
  • You want the person who audits your site to be the person who fixes it.
  • You want to spend your budget on SEO work rather than on account management and overhead.
  • You prefer month-to-month terms over a long commitment.

THE HONEST PART

What I can't do (and why I'm telling you)

  • I don't run paid media or social campaigns at scale. If you need them, I'll recommend a specialist.

  • If I'm ill or unavailable, work can slow. To reduce that risk, I document everything in reports and sheets you keep — your SEO never depends on my memory.
