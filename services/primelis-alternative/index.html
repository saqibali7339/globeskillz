The Alternative

Big-agency polish. Freelance economics.

Large SEO agencies do good work for enterprise clients — but their model has costs: layered account teams, standardized playbooks applied to every client, junior execution, and pricing that reflects Mayfair or Manhattan offices. If you’re a growing business rather than a multinational, you end up paying for machinery you don’t need.

I’m built for the businesses in between: ambitious enough to need serious SEO, smart enough not to overpay for it. You get senior strategy tailored to your market — not a playbook — executed personally, with direct access and transparent reporting. The polish of a good agency deliverable, at the economics of working directly with the expert.
