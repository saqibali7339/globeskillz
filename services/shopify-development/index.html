What “SEO-ready” actually means

  • Collections that match how people search. The structure comes from search data, not from how your warehouse is organized.
  • One canonical URL per product, with internal links pointing to it.
  • A real heading structure on every template: one H1, with sensible H2s under it.
  • Content areas on collections, so you can add copy without asking a developer.
  • Fast by default: lazy-loaded media, critical CSS, and a small number of apps.

FAQ

Shopify Store Development questions

Have a different question? Ask me on WhatsApp.

How much does a Shopify store build cost?

Builds start at $400 for a theme-based store with 5–8 customized templates. Custom themes, complex catalogs and multi-market setups are quoted after a discovery call.

Do you design from scratch or customize a theme?

Either. For most stores a well-chosen premium theme with custom sections is the fastest and most cost-effective option. Fully custom themes are best when your brand or catalog needs them.

Will I be able to edit the store myself?

Yes. Everything is built with Online Store 2.0 sections and blocks, and you get a short video guide for your team.

Do you build on platforms other than Shopify?

My focus is Shopify. I’ve built on WooCommerce and WordPress in the past, but new development work is Shopify-only, so every build gets my full platform expertise.
