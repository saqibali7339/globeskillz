The service

What I actually do for you.

01 / Google Business Profile

Your new storefront.

Your Business Profile is the first thing local searchers see — before your website. I optimize every field, category, service, and photo; set up posts and Q&A; and build a review strategy that keeps fresh 5-star reviews flowing. A neglected profile leaks customers daily. A tuned one prints them.

02 / Citations + location pages

Be everywhere local search looks.

I clean up and build your citations across the directories Google trusts, fix the inconsistent name/address/phone data that's confusing the algorithm, and create location pages on your site that rank for ‘[service] in [city]’ searches. Every signal pointing the same way: you're the obvious local choice.
