How I approach AI Search Optimization

Written by Saqib Ali, Shopify SEO lead & developer at Globe Skillz. Updated November 2026.

Saqib Ali
Shopify SEO & development since 2019. 180+ projects across 42 countries.

AI search is built on top of regular SEO

AI answers are built from pages that already rank and sources that are already trusted. There’s no separate trick. What helps is being very clear about who you are and what you sell, being easy for these systems to quote, and getting mentioned on the sites they read.

What changes on your store

  • Collection and product pages open with a plain one-sentence summary
  • Specs, materials and comparisons go into tables instead of long paragraphs
  • One About page that’s the authoritative source of brand facts
  • Consistent schema across every template, linked with @id
Honest note: AI visibility is newer and harder to measure than rankings. I report what can be measured, which is prompt checks and AI-referral traffic in GA4, and I don’t promise placements.

FAQ

AI Search Optimization questions

Have a different question? Ask me on WhatsApp.

What is GEO or AEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) both mean optimizing so AI assistants and AI Overviews mention and cite your brand. In practice it’s SEO with extra focus on entity clarity, structured data and quotable content.

Can I pay to appear in ChatGPT answers?

Not in organic answers. Visibility comes from being a trusted, well-described and well-cited source, which is what this service works on.

Should I block AI crawlers?

That’s a business decision. Blocking them protects your content but removes you from AI answers. For most stores that want to be found, allowing them is the better choice. I’ll explain the trade-offs.

Is this included in your monthly Shopify SEO?

The basics are included in every retainer. The standalone service adds deeper content restructuring, third-party outreach and prompt tracking.
