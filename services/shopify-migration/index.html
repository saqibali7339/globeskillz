How I approach Migration to Shopify

Why migrations lose traffic

Shopify uses fixed URL patterns: /products/, /collections/, /pages/ and /blogs/news/. Your old platform almost certainly used different ones. Every ranked URL that isn’t redirected to its closest equivalent loses its rankings and the value of its backlinks.

The migration sequence

  1. Benchmark: record rankings, traffic and top pages before anything changes
  2. Inventory and redirect map, approved by you
  3. Build and migrate on a development store
  4. Pre-launch crawl of the development store against the old site
  5. Launch, then redirect upload and immediate verification
  6. Eight weeks of monitoring and fixes

FAQ

Migration to Shopify (SEO-Safe) questions

Have a different question? Ask me on WhatsApp.

Will I lose SEO rankings moving to Shopify?

A short dip in the first 2–4 weeks is normal while Google processes the redirects. With a complete redirect map and matching content, most stores recover within 4–8 weeks and often improve after that, because Shopify is faster.

Can I keep my URLs exactly the same?

Not entirely, because Shopify’s URL prefixes are fixed. Product handles can usually stay the same, so the only change is the prefix, which keeps redirects clean.

Do you migrate customers and orders?

Yes. Customers, order history, products, reviews and blog content can all be moved. Customer passwords can’t be transferred, so I set up an account-activation email.

Can you redesign during the migration?

Yes, and it’s often the most efficient approach. The SEO checks stay the same, and content parity on key pages becomes even more important.
