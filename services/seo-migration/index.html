SEO Migration

Most migrations lose traffic. Yours won’t.

A website migration is the single riskiest moment in SEO. Redesigns, platform switches, domain changes, URL restructuring — every one of them is a chance for Google to lose trust in your site. And it happens constantly: the new site looks beautiful, the redirects were ‘handled’ by the dev team, and three weeks later organic traffic is down 40% and nobody knows why.

I treat migrations like surgery: planned in detail, executed carefully, monitored obsessively. Before launch I crawl your existing site and map every URL that matters. I audit the staging environment and catch the issues that would have cost you rankings. After launch I watch your traffic and rankings daily until we’re stable. Boring? Absolutely. That’s the point — boring migrations are the ones that work.

The service

What I actually do for you: SEO migration.

01 / Before launch

Every URL mapped.

I crawl your entire current site — every indexed page, every page with backlinks, every page that drives traffic — and build a complete redirect map. Each old URL gets a proper 301 to its closest match on the new site. No chains, no redirect loops, no ‘we’ll figure it out after launch.’ I also check content parity: if the redesign thins out important pages, we fix it before Google ever sees it.

02 / After launch

Watched like a hawk.

Launch day isn’t the finish line, it’s the start of the danger zone. I monitor your rankings, traffic, and indexation daily for 30 days post-migration. Crawl errors get fixed the same day they appear. If anything looks wrong, we act immediately — and yes, there’s a rollback plan ready before we ever go live, just in case.

Deliverables

Everything included in SEO migration, nothing hidden.

  • Complete redirect map — every old URL gets a proper 301 to its closest match. No chains, no 404s.

Process

How SEO migration works.

STEP 1

Migration risk audit

STEP 2

Redirect + parity map

STEP 3

Staging validation

The new site gets crawled on staging. Technical issues, missing content, redirect errors — all fixed before a single visitor arrives.

Launch + monitor
