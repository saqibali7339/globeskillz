Common Shopify Markets mistakes

  • Leaving auto-redirects by IP on, so Googlebot (crawling from the US) never sees your other markets
  • Translated content with English handles and English meta descriptions
  • Country subfolders with identical content and no reason to exist
  • Missing x-default, which leaves Google to guess

FAQ

International Shopify SEO questions

Have a different question? Ask me on WhatsApp.

Should I use subfolders or separate domains for international Shopify SEO?

For most brands, subfolders (example.com/en-gb/) on a single store are best, because every market shares the same domain authority. Separate domains make sense when markets need very different catalogs or brands.

Does Shopify Markets handle hreflang automatically?

Mostly, yes. It still needs checking: custom themes, apps and translated handles can break it, and x-default and return tags often need adjusting.

Do I need to translate everything?

Start with the pages that earn revenue: home, top collections, top products and policies. Translate the blog selectively, based on keyword demand in each market.

Can you handle Pakistan or other emerging markets?

Yes. Search behavior and competition are different in each market, and I adjust the keyword research and content to match.
