AI Search (AEO / GEO)

Search changed. Your strategy should too.

Millions of people now ask AI instead of googling. ChatGPT, Google’s AI Overviews, and Perplexity answer directly — and each answer cites only a handful of sources. If your business isn’t one of them, you don’t just lose a click. You lose the entire conversation, because the user never sees page one at all.

AI doesn’t rank pages the way Google does — it assembles answers from sources it trusts: clear entities, factual Q&A content, structured data, and brands with real authority signals. I optimize your entire presence for how large language models actually pick sources, so when buyers ask AI who to hire, your business is the name in the answer.

The service

What I actually optimize for AI.

01 / AEO: win the direct answer

Own the answer box, everywhere.

Answer-engine optimization makes your content the source AI pulls from: question-led pages, quotable facts, FAQ schema, and formatting that large language models can lift cleanly. When AI answers a question in your market, it answers with your words.

02 / GEO: get cited by generative AI

Become the brand AI trusts.

Generative-engine optimization builds the entity behind the answers: a defined brand across the web, consistent facts everywhere you’re mentioned, llms.txt for AI crawlers, reviews and citations that prove you’re real. AI cites businesses it can verify — I make you verifiable.

Deliverables

What’s covered in every AI search engagement.

  • Entity and brand optimization — your business defined clearly and consistently across the web.
  • Answer-ready Q&A content — pages written so AI can extract clean, quotable answers.
  • Schema markup — FAQ, Service, Organization, and LocalBusiness structured data AI relies on.
  • llms.txt and AI crawler access — a machine-readable map of your site for ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity crawlers.
  • Authority signals — reviews, citations, and mentions that make AI trust your business is real and reputable.
  • AI visibility tracking — monitoring where and how AI platforms mention you, month after month.
