SEO Audits

You can’t fix what you can’t see.

Most site owners I talk to are doing ‘some SEO’ — a plugin here, a few keywords there — while something structural quietly bleeds their traffic. A robots.txt blocking half the site. Hundreds of pages Google never indexed. A competitor eating their lunch with content they could have written in a weekend. You can't feel these problems from inside your business. That's what the audit is for.

I crawl your entire site the way Google does, compare you against the competitors outranking you, and give you a straight answer: here's what's broken, here's what matters most, and here's what to do first. You'll know exactly where your money should go — whether you spend it with me or not.

The service

What I actually do for you: SEO audits.

01 / What I check

Everything Google cares about.

Technical crawl of every URL, indexation and crawl-budget analysis, Core Web Vitals, on-page optimization, content gaps versus your top three competitors, backlink profile health, and local SEO factors if you serve an area. Over 200 checkpoints — but you won't see a checklist. You'll see a story: what's wrong, why it matters, and what it costs you.

02 / What you receive

A plan, not a document.

My audits end with a prioritized action list, not a data dump. Quick wins you can do this week, medium fixes for this month, and the big strategic moves for this quarter — each with the expected impact spelled out. Then we get on a call and I walk you through the whole thing, so you can ask me anything and challenge anything.

Deliverables

What’s inside every audit.

  • Full technical crawl — every page, every redirect, every error, mapped the way Googlebot sees it.
  • Indexation analysis — the pages Google ignores and the junk pages wasting your crawl budget.
  • Content gap report — the keywords your competitors rank for that you don't, with the easiest wins highlighted.
  • Backlink health check — toxic links, lost links, and what your competitors' link profiles reveal.
  • Core Web Vitals review — exactly which pages fail, why, and what fixing them is worth.
  • 30-minute walkthrough call — I explain every finding live and answer everything. No jargon allowed.
Process

How your audit happens.

STEP 1

Access + goals

You give me Search Console access and tell me what a customer is worth to you. That number decides how I prioritize everything.

STEP 2

Deep crawl

I crawl your site, pull your Search Console and backlink data, and dissect your top three competitors. About two days of proper work.

STEP 3

Plain-English report

You get the prioritized action list — quick wins first, each with expected impact. Written for a business owner, not an SEO.

STEP 4

Walkthrough call

We go through it together for 30 minutes. Ask me anything. By the end you'll know exactly what to do next.
