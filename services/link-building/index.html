Link Building

Links are still half the ranking battle.

You can have the best content in your industry and still sit on page three, because Google treats backlinks as votes — and right now your competitors have more votes. Buying cheap link packages feels like a shortcut until Google catches the pattern, and then you're not on page three, you're nowhere. I've seen it happen. It's an expensive lesson.

I earn links one by one from real, relevant websites — the kind with actual traffic and actual readers. It's slower than the spammy packages, and that's the point: these links stick, they compound, and they never put your site at risk. Every link I build is one I'd be comfortable showing Google.

The service

What I actually do for you: link building.

01 / How I earn links

Outreach, not automation.

I find websites your customers actually read, pitch them genuinely useful content or resources, and earn placements that make sense. Guest posts on real blogs, resource-page inclusions, broken-link replacements, and digital PR angles when your business has a story worth telling. Every prospect goes on a list you approve before I contact anyone — no surprises, ever.

02 / What I will never do

The stuff that gets sites penalized.

No PBNs. No link farms. No paid link schemes dressed up as ‘guest posts’ on sites that exist only to sell links. No spammy directories, no footer links, no automated blog comments. If a tactic would embarrass you to explain to Google, I won't do it. This costs me the clients who want 500 links for $50 — good. Those links don't work anyway.

Deliverables

How I keep it clean and effective.

  • Manual outreach only — every email written by a human, to a human, about something genuinely relevant.
  • You approve the prospect list — nothing gets pitched without your sign-off. Your brand, your rules.
  • Real sites with real traffic — I check traffic, relevance, and link quality before anything goes on the list.
  • Natural anchor text strategy — branded, naked, and topical anchors mixed the way real links look.
  • Links you keep — everything I build is yours permanently, even if we stop working together.
  • Transparent monthly reporting — every live link, its metrics, and what it cost. No black box.
Process

How your links get built.

STEP 1

Backlink gap analysis

I compare your link profile against your top competitors and show you exactly where the gaps are. Free, before you commit to anything.

STEP 2

Prospect list you approve

I build a list of real, relevant sites worth approaching. You review it, veto anything, and only then do I start outreach.

STEP 3

Outreach + placement

I pitch, follow up, and negotiate placements personally. You hear about links as they go live — not as a surprise spreadsheet at month's end.

STEP 4

Report + compound

Monthly report with every link and its metrics. Then we double down on what worked and expand into the next tier of targets.
