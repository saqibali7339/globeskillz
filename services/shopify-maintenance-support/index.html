Why pair support with SEO

Many SEO setbacks start with a small developer change: an app that adds a second H1, a theme update that drops the schema, or a new filter that creates thousands of indexable URLs. Because support and SEO sit with one person here, these problems get caught before they go live.

“I told him we had a deadline and delivered before the deadline was met… 10/10 will be using again, very affordable service.”

How it works

  • Send tasks on WhatsApp, by email or in a shared board
  • Each task gets an estimate before work starts
  • Work happens on a duplicate theme, with a preview link for you to approve
  • A monthly summary lists what changed and how many hours remain

Shopify Maintenance & Support questions

Have a different question? Ask me on WhatsApp.

What does Shopify maintenance include?

Bug fixes, small feature builds, theme updates, testing new apps, and a monthly health check of speed, 404s and structured data, all within your monthly hours.

What if I need more hours one month?

Extra hours are billed at the same rate, and I’ll always ask first. Unused hours roll over for one month.

Can support be combined with an SEO retainer?

Yes. Most SEO clients add a support block so that technical recommendations get implemented right away.

Which timezone do you work in?

I’m based in Pakistan (PKT, UTC+5) and work with clients worldwide. We agree on a daily overlap window during onboarding.
