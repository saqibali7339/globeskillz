Technical SEO

Google can’t rank what it can’t crawl.

Here's the uncomfortable truth: you can publish brilliant content every week and still go nowhere if your technical foundation is cracked. Pages Google never discovers. Half your crawl budget wasted on filter URLs. A mobile layout that fails Core Web Vitals on every page. These problems are invisible from inside your business — your site looks fine to you. It does not look fine to Googlebot.

I audit your site the way a search engine sees it — raw crawl data, Search Console, page experience signals — then I fix what's broken and verify the fix with real data. Not ‘we improved things.’ Numbers: indexed pages up, errors down, vitals passing. That's the job.

The service

What I actually do for you.

01 / What I fix

The unsexy stuff that moves rankings.

Crawlability and indexation, XML sitemaps and robots.txt, Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS), mobile usability issues, canonicalization and duplicate content, hreflang for multilingual sites, structured data and schema, redirect chains and migration handling, 404 and soft-404 cleanup, site architecture and internal linking. If it's between Google and your content, it's my territory.

02 / How I prove it worked

Data, not promises.

Every engagement starts with a baseline: indexed page counts, crawl stats, vitals scores, error counts. After fixes, I re-crawl and pull fresh Search Console data, and you see the before and after side by side. If a fix didn't move the number, I tell you that too — then we figure out why. You'll never have to take my word for anything.

Deliverables

What’s covered in every engagement.

  • Full-site crawl — every URL analyzed, not a sample. Big sites welcome.
  • Core Web Vitals remediation — I find the exact failing elements and fix them, not just report them.
  • Indexation cleanup — noindex bloat removed, valuable pages pushed into the index, crawl budget reclaimed.
  • Structured data implementation — schema that earns rich results, validated, not copy-pasted.
  • Migration safety — replatforms and redesigns handled without the traffic cliff. 301 maps, verified.
  • Search Console verification — every fix confirmed with Google's own data before I call it done.
Process

How your fixes get done.

STEP 1

Deep-dive diagnosis

Full crawl plus your Search Console data. I map every technical issue and rank them by traffic impact — not by how scary they sound.

STEP 2

Fix plan you approve

You get the prioritized list with plain-English explanations. You approve what gets fixed and in what order. No surprise changes to your live site.

STEP 3

Implementation

I implement the fixes myself on WordPress/Shopify, or work directly with your developer if you have one. Staging first for anything risky.

STEP 4

Verification with data

Re-crawl, fresh Search Console pulls, before-and-after numbers. You see exactly what changed and what it was worth.
