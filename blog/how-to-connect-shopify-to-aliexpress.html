How to Connect Shopify to AliExpress for Dropshipping

Want to sell AliExpress products on your Shopify store? Here is how to connect the two, import products, and start dropshipping the right way.

Connecting Shopify to AliExpress is how thousands of new store owners start dropshipping. You list products on your store without buying stock first, and when a customer orders, the supplier ships the item straight to them.

It sounds simple, and the connection itself is. But most beginners get it wrong by importing random products with no system. This guide walks you through the connection step by step, then covers the practical details that keep a dropshipping store out of trouble: picking products, setting prices, handling shipping times, and dealing with returns.

Step 1: Install the DSers app on Shopify

The quickest way to connect Shopify and AliExpress is through the DSers app, which is the official dropshipping tool AliExpress recommends. In your Shopify admin, go to Apps, click Visit the Shopify App Store, and search for DSers. Install it and create an account inside the app.

Once installed, link your AliExpress account to DSers. You do this from the DSers dashboard by signing in with the AliExpress account you want to use for sourcing. This link is what lets the app pull product details and later place orders on your behalf.

After the two accounts are linked, pick your plan. DSers offers a free plan that is enough to get started: it lets you import products, set pricing rules, and place orders. You can always upgrade when order volume grows.

Step 2: Import products and clean them up

With the connection live, you can start importing products. Use the DSers browser extension or the app dashboard to browse AliExpress, and when you find a product you want to sell, click import. The product lands in your DSers import list, not your store yet, which gives you a chance to clean it up first.

This cleanup step matters. AliExpress product titles are often keyword-stuffed and unreadable. Rewrite the title in plain language your customer would search for. Replace the supplier photos with the best ones, or upload your own if you have samples. Write a description that speaks to the buyer, not a copy of the supplier text.

Then push the product to Shopify. DSers lets you assign it to a collection, set the product type and tags, and map the variants (size, color) so they appear correctly on your store.

Step 3: Set your prices with pricing rules

DSers has pricing rules that automatically set your retail price when a product is imported. The most common approach for beginners is a fixed multiplier: cost multiplied by 2 or 2.5. So a product that costs you $8 from the supplier sells for $16 to $20 on your store.

Do not blindly apply one multiplier to everything. Cheaper items need a higher multiple to cover fees and ads, while expensive items can survive on a lower one. Check your final margin after Shopify fees and payment processing, and make sure each sale leaves enough profit to matter.

Set a consistent free-shipping threshold if you can. It encourages larger orders and simplifies the checkout. Just build the average shipping cost into your product prices first.

Step 4: Fulfill orders through the app

When a customer buys from your store, the order appears in DSers. You click to place the order on AliExpress, pay the supplier, and the supplier ships it to your customer. This is the one click fulfillment that makes dropshipping work without you touching inventory.

DSers can place orders in bulk, which saves time as volume grows. You can also assign different suppliers to different products and let the app track which order goes where. Keep an eye on stock: if a supplier runs out of a variant, your store listing should be paused or the variant removed.

Always check the tracking number the supplier provides and make sure it shows movement within a few days. If tracking stalls, contact the supplier before your customer has to ask you.

Step 5: Get shipping times under control

AliExpress shipping is the part that trips up most new dropshippers. Standard shipping to many countries takes 10 to 20 days, which surprises customers used to fast delivery. Your job is to set honest expectations on the product page instead of hiding the timeline.

Look for products that offer faster options. Many suppliers show local warehouse delivery, which cuts shipping to under a week in the US and parts of Europe. ePacket-style tracked shipping is another safer choice over the cheapest untracked methods.

Test the shipping yourself before you scale any product. Order a sample to your own address and time it. If it takes 25 days, you know exactly what to tell customers, and you can decide whether the product is worth selling.

Step 6: Plan for returns before they happen

Returns work differently in dropshipping because you do not hold the stock. The practical approach: handle returns and refunds yourself and build the cost into your pricing. Do not ask customers to ship faulty items back to China.

Write your policy before your first sale. A simple, fair policy is a full refund for items that never arrive after a reasonable window, and a 30-day window for faulty items with either a replacement or refund. Clear policy pages also reduce chargebacks.

Choose suppliers with high ratings and a track record of accepting dispute resolutions. AliExpress buyer protection gives you leverage when a supplier ships the wrong item, but only if you file within the protection window.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an official way to connect AliExpress to Shopify?

Yes, you can connect them with the official DSers app from the Shopify App Store. It is built for AliExpress dropshipping and handles product imports, pricing rules, and one-click order fulfillment.

Do I need to pay for a dropshipping app?

The standard DSers plan is free and covers enough for most beginners, including basic product imports and order placement. Higher-volume stores can upgrade later, but you do not need a paid plan to start.

How long does AliExpress shipping take for dropshipping?

Delivery times depend on the supplier and shipping method. Many sellers offer 10 to 15 day delivery to the US and UK. Look for products with local warehouse options in the product description for faster shipping.

How do I handle returns when I dropship from AliExpress?

Set a clear policy before your first sale: a full refund for items that never arrive, and a 30-day return window for faulty items. Since suppliers vary, handle returns yourself and factor the cost into your pricing.
