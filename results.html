How to read these numbers

Clicks are visits from Google Search. Impressions count how often the store appeared in results. Average position is weighted across every query, so a store ranking first for its main terms can still average 15–20 if it also shows up for thousands of long-tail queries. Position matters most alongside clicks.

Results depend on the niche, the starting point, budget and product. I don’t guarantee rankings, and you should be wary of anyone who does. What I can promise is honest work and reporting from your own Search Console data.
