About

Hi, I’m Saqib Ali — SEO Specialist and Shopify developer.

I’m Saqib Ali, an SEO Specialist from Gojra, Punjab, Pakistan. SEO is my main craft — the work I’ve done for 5+ years and the niche I love — and my background is in WordPress and Shopify development. So far, I’ve helped 15+ businesses rank #1 on Google.

Globe Skillz and me

Globe Skillz is my own studio, and this website is my own work — not an agency’s. I founded it in 2019. Today it focuses on one niche: Shopify — SEO first, with development built in to support it.

How I work

  1. Free store review. You send your store URL. Within one business day, you get a short recorded review of the biggest SEO and technical issues I can find.
  2. Prioritized plan. I audit the store and hand you a fix list in priority order — what moves rankings first, not a 100-page report.
  3. Fixes in your theme. I make the code changes myself: technical SEO, Liquid, speed, schema. No handoff to another developer.
  4. Content and links. Guides that sell, and authority links that stick — built around the keywords your buyers actually search.
  5. Reporting from your Search Console. You see real clicks, impressions and positions from your own account — not screenshots of someone else’s dashboard.

Why choose me

  • One person, end to end. Strategy and code from the same hands — nothing gets lost between an SEO and a developer.
  • Shopify-only focus. I know this platform’s quirks: duplicate product paths, tag pages, Markets, app bloat — and how to fix them fast.
  • Proven rankings. 15+ businesses ranked #1, built over 5+ years in SEO.
  • White-hat only. No PBNs, no doorway pages — safe through Google updates.
  • Direct line. You talk to me on WhatsApp, not to an account manager.
  • Your data, your accounts. Everything I build belongs to you.

Who I work with

Shopify merchants worldwide, from new DTC brands to established catalogs.
