The couple's journey

It starts with a ring and a search bar.

She says yes on a Saturday. By Sunday night there is a shared note called wedding, forty open tabs, and a first search typed into Google. Nobody hires a wedding vendor without searching first anymore. Not the venue, not the photographer, not the florist. The whole wedding industry now runs on a sequence of searches, and the vendors who show up first get the shortlist.

It happens in an order. First the venue, because the date depends on it. Then the planner, if they want one. Then the photographer and videographer, because the good ones book out a year ahead. Then florals, the DJ, the rentals, and finally the fun extras like a photo booth. Each search is emotional and high ticket. Couples decide fast once they feel sure, and they feel sure about the vendor whose site answered everything: real photos, honest prices, recent reviews, and a booking path that takes seconds.

That is what SEO for wedding businesses really is. Not tricks. Making sure that when a couple searches for what you do, in the city you serve, you are the obvious choice.

01 · Venues

The venue gets booked first. Everyone else fights for the leftovers.

Directories like The Knot and WeddingWire put you in front of browsing couples fast, but you sit next to every competitor, the enquiry goes to five venues at once, and you pay every single month. It is rented visibility. When your own site ranks for wedding venues in your city, the couple lands on your gallery, your pricing, your tour form. No competitors beside you, no per lead fees. Keep one directory for overflow and invest the rest in rankings that compound.

The searches that book tours are brutally specific: outdoor wedding venues near me, vineyard wedding venue, wedding venue with accommodation. Each deserves its own landing page with photos of that exact offering. And seasonality runs the business: couples search hardest from December to March, so availability and seasonal pricing pages published early capture that wave, while winter wedding content fills your quiet dates.

Treat your Google Business Profile like a brochure you update monthly: fresh real wedding photos, open day posts, and the questions section answered in your own words before strangers answer for you.

Planners and photographers send you clients too, and vendor cross linking between your sites lifts everyone involved.

  • One landing page per space and style. A barn, a garden pavilion and a ballroom are three different searches.
  • Seasonal pages published a quarter early, so they are ranking when the December to March wave hits.
  • Your profile is a second brochure: real wedding photos, correct categories, answered questions.
02 · Planners

Couples hire the planner they find in ninety seconds.

It is Saturday morning and an engaged couple types wedding planner near me into Google. Three planners show up with photos, glowing reviews and a booking link. By Sunday night they have paid a deposit to someone else. That ninety second search, months before they ever speak to you, is where most planner clients are won now.

Couples search in three waves: dreaming, shortlisting, then hiring. The hiring wave is where the money is: best wedding planner in the city, luxury wedding planner near me. Each wave needs its own page, written the way a stressed, excited couple actually types, on a site that loads fast and answers pricing in thirty seconds.

The three pages most planner sites are missing: a pricing page with honest ranges that pre qualifies enquiries, one page per wedding type you want more of (multicultural, LGBTQ, elopements, destination), and real wedding features naming the venue and city. Those features rank for venue plus planner searches and earn you backlinks from venues, the strongest links a planner can get.

  • A pricing page with real ranges outranks ten pretty galleries and scares off only the wrong couples.
  • Dedicated pages for the two or three wedding types you want more of, each with matching photos.
  • Every wedding you plan becomes a search asset that earns venue backlinks.
03 · Wedding photography

Your photos do the selling. Google just can't see them.

Here is the cruel irony of wedding photography SEO: the thing that sells your work is invisible to Google. It sees filenames like IMG_4829.jpg, empty alt text, and a gallery page with twelve words on it. My first job is translating your visual talent into signals Google can rank: descriptive filenames, alt text, written real wedding features naming the venue and city, and location pages for the areas you actually shoot.

You live off two search types. Wedding photographer near me is map driven and review driven, and the reviews need to mention weddings specifically. Style searches like documentary wedding photographer are where the high value clients hide, and almost nobody optimizes for them.

One more thing couples check: you. An about page with your photo, your approach and a short video outconverts a logo and a contact form every time, because they are hiring the person who will shadow them all day.

  • Descriptive filenames and alt text turn your gallery into something Google can actually rank.
  • One real wedding feature per wedding, naming the venue, earns backlinks money cannot buy.
  • Style pages capture the high budget clients that generic photographer pages miss.
04 · Portrait and family photography

Specialists get found. Generalists get scrolled past.

Photographer in your city is one of the most competitive local searches there is. A site with a bit of everything, weddings, newborns, headshots, real estate, spreads thin and ranks for nothing. Google rewards clear focus, and so do clients: specialists charge more because expertise reads as premium.

I look at what you love shooting, what pays best per hour, and where local competition is weakest. The overlap is your lane, whether that is family sessions, newborns, or engagement shoots that feed your wedding book. Then every page, gallery and review points the same direction.

Location pages get built only where you have proof: real shoots in that town, venue names, genuine travel information. Five strong location pages beat twenty template pages, and your enquiry form gets simplified to date and venue first, because rankings bring traffic but the enquiry path decides how much becomes bookings.

  • Build the site around your one or two most profitable genres. You can still accept other work.
  • Location pages only where you have real shoots to show. Proof beats templates every time.
  • A short form asking date and venue first converts browsers that a long form would lose.
05 · Videography

Your films close the deal. Words get them watched.

The paradox of videography SEO: your showreel persuades, but Google cannot watch it. It sees an embedded video, eighty words of text, and meaningless filenames. I bridge that gap. Every film gets a page with a real title, a few hundred words about the project, the location, the client type and the style, while the video underneath does what video does best.

Do not forget the buyers comparing philosophies: searches like documentary wedding film style reveal couples choosing an approach, not just a price. A process page explaining how you work ranks for those comparisons and pre sells your style before the enquiry.

And your YouTube channel is a second search engine most videographers underuse. Titles, descriptions and buyer focused playlists optimized around the same keywords as your site mean the two reinforce each other.

  • Every film gets its own page with context Google can rank and couples can fall for.
  • An organized, keyword titled YouTube channel becomes a second pipeline, not a portfolio dump.
  • Honest pricing pages capture cost searches your competitors are afraid to touch.
06 · Florists

Two customers, one shop: impulse buyers and wedding budgets.

You serve two completely different customers. The first buys on impulse: birthday flowers ordered on a phone in five minutes, straight from the map pack. The second plans for months: wedding florals with consultations, mood boards and four figure budgets. One site, two funnels, no confusion between them.

Weddings are the high ticket. A single wedding booking can equal a month of daily orders, and wedding florist searches are far less competitive than flower delivery terms. Portfolio pages per wedding style, each with real photos and honest budget guidance, rank well and earn links from planners and photographers.

Your toughest competitors are not other local shops. They are national order gatherers that rank for your city, take the order, and pass it to a local florist at a discount. The defense is local depth they cannot fake: real shop photos, neighborhood delivery pages, local reviews, and owning every variation of your own business name.

  • Separate paths for impulse orders and wedding consultations, each with its own call to action.
  • Wedding portfolio pages per style earn vendor backlinks that lift your whole site.
  • Beat order gatherers with genuine local signals they cannot copy.
07 · DJs

Before the shortlist, there's a search bar.

Here is how DJ hiring really works. A couple gets engaged, asks married friends for recommendations, Googles two or three names, and books whoever looks most professional online. If your site looks dated next to a competitor with video, reviews and clear pricing, the friend recommendation dies right there.

The searches that book gigs are specific: wedding DJ near me, DJ for corporate event, bilingual wedding DJ, DJ and lighting packages. Each is a different buyer with a different budget. Weddings and corporate events get separate pages, because one page trying to speak to both converts neither.

You have a content advantage most trades would kill for: every gig produces media. A two minute highlight reel embedded on a city service page with real context is an asset competitors cannot copy. And venue specific pages start ranking for venue plus DJ searches, which turns coordinators into referral sources.

  • Monthly highlight reels with real written context build a library nobody can replicate.
  • Venue specific pages rank for venue plus DJ searches and earn coordinator referrals.
  • Separate wedding and corporate pages. Different buyers, different budgets, different searches.
08 · Party rentals

Search has a season. Your rankings should too.

Party rental is one of the most seasonal searches in local SEO. Tent and wedding rental queries surge from March to June, graduation rentals spike in April and May, holiday party searches climb through November. Google needs weeks to trust a new page, so if you publish your graduation tent page in April, you have already lost April. We build seasonal pages a full quarter early.

Every major inventory category deserves its own page that reads like a mini catalog: photos of the actual items, sizes and capacities, setup requirements, honest pricing guidance. Tent pages say which sizes fit which guest counts. Add on searches like dance floor rental and lighting rental are high intent and low competition, often booked alongside the main order.

And rental enquiries die in slow quote forms. Item selectors, date pickers, instant estimates where possible, click to call everywhere on mobile. Rankings bring the traffic. The quote path turns it into booked weekends.

  • Seasonal landing pages go live a quarter early, ranking exactly when the phones should ring.
  • Every inventory category gets a mini catalog page with photos, sizes and pricing guidance.
  • Streamlined quote forms with item selectors turn searches into booked weekends.
09 · Photo booths

The add-on search almost nobody competes for.

Couples search photo booth rental for wedding in the months before the big day. Corporate planners search photo booth for corporate event all year round. The corporate side is the hidden goldmine: higher budgets, repeat annual events, and far less competition than wedding terms. Most booth companies ignore it completely.

Then there is the third wave: the add on search. Someone books a venue, then searches photo booth add on for wedding. Someone hires a DJ, then searches photo booth with DJ package. Lower volume, almost no competition, and the buyer is already spending.

Your money pages are specific: 360 photo booth rental in your city, mirror photo booth, wedding photo booth packages, each with pricing, photos of that exact setup, and a quote form above the fold. And because planners browse on phones between meetings, your gallery has to load instantly or they bounce to the next result.

  • Target the add on searches where buyers are already spending and competition is near zero.
  • One page per booth type with video of the setup converts far better than a homepage bullet.
  • Corporate pages unlock bigger budgets and repeat annual bookings.
