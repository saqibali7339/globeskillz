THE VERIFICATION GAUNTLET

Four questions stand between you and the call.

Are you qualified?Credentials mean nothing if Google does not show them. Your qualifications need their own visible page, not a line in an about blurb.
Do you know my industry?“Bookkeeping for agencies” beats “small business bookkeeping” every time. Buyers pay for someone who has seen their chaos before.
What does it cost?Vague pricing sends cautious buyers back to Google. A clear starting price filters for serious enquiries and wins the comparison.
Can I trust you with my numbers?Reviews are references. No reviews means no references — and a careful buyer will not be your first.
