Straight answers

Do I really need to care about Seznam?

Short answer: yes, but not the way most agencies tell you. As an SEO expert in Czechia, here are the questions Czech business owners actually ask me, answered honestly. If your question is not on the list, the free audit answers it with your own numbers instead of guesses.

Question 01

Is Seznam still a thing?

Yes. Google leads in Czechia, but Seznam holds a meaningful share, especially among older users and for certain local queries. The practical implication is small but real: claim and complete your Firmy.cz listing (Seznam's business directory), because it feeds visibility on Seznam's results and map. It takes an afternoon and most competitors skip it.

Question 02

Can I rank in Czechia with an English website?

For local customers, no. Czechs search in Czech, Google.cz ranks Czech pages for Czech queries, and Czech customers click Czech results. English works for the Prague tourist trade and export B2B, but a local business needs Czech pages built from real Czech search terms.

Question 03

Is Czech keyword research really different?

Completely. Czech morphology means one word appears in many forms, and searchers use the forms that feel natural, not the dictionary form. Translated English keywords miss most of the real volume. I build Czech keyword maps from Czech search data, which is slower and is exactly why it works.

Question 04

What about reviews? Do Czechs read them?

Yes, and Google reviews plus Firmy.cz ratings both matter. Czech buyers are sceptical of marketing claims and trust peer proof. A business with fifty genuine Czech reviews will beat a better-optimised site with five, because the reviews feed both the ranking and the click.

Two cities

Prague gets the attention. Brno gets the engineers.

Prague

The capital dominates search volume and competition. Tourism adds a whole second keyword economy: hotels, restaurants, experiences, all with sharp seasonality. Local services compete with chains and well-funded players. Prague rewards depth: the best Czech content, the most complete profile, the strongest review base. It is the hardest market in the country and the biggest prize.

Brno and Moravia

Brno anchors the Moravian tech and engineering scene with real B2B search demand and far less SEO competition than Prague. Manufacturing heritage means strong search behaviour around industrial services, suppliers, and technical trades. For B2B businesses, Brno is often the most efficient market in Czechia: serious buyers, thin competition.

Smaller cities like Ostrava, Plzen, and Olomouc follow the same pattern with even less competition. And across the border, the dynamics rhyme: see how I think about SEO in Slovakia or SEO in Poland for the regional picture.

Days 1 to 20: audit and listings. Full technical audit, Czech keyword map, and your listings claimed everywhere that matters: Google Business Profile and Firmy.cz first, then the rest. Most Czech businesses have never seen their own audit; the list is usually eye-opening.

Seasonality

The Prague tourist wave is a strategy, not luck.

If your business touches tourism, restaurants, hotels, experiences, transport, your year has a shape: quiet winter, building spring, intense summer, golden autumn. The businesses that win the summer do their SEO in winter. Content published in January ranks by June; a profile optimised in February collects reviews all summer.

I plan tourism SEO backwards from the season. We decide which summer keywords matter, build the pages and profiles months ahead, and let them mature while competitors are still thinking about it. Seasonal markets punish procrastination and reward planners, and the planning starts the day after the season ends.
