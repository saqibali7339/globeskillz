Local SEO · Canada

Two languages, ten provinces, one strategy that covers them.

Canada looks like one market on a map and behaves like several. Toronto’s search landscape has more in common with Chicago than with Halifax; Montreal runs substantially in French; Vancouver’s buyers think Pacific Rim. A single “Canadian” SEO template misses all of this — and most of your competitors are running exactly that template.

The French factor is the biggest open secret in Canadian SEO. A huge share of Quebec searches happen in French, and English-only businesses forfeit most of that market without realizing it. Real French content — written for Quebec search habits, not translated as an afterthought — opens a demand channel with far less competition than English Toronto.

As an SEO expert in Canada, I build province-aware campaigns: English-first where English dominates, genuinely bilingual where it matters, and review and citation strategies tuned to how Canadians actually buy — carefully, thoroughly, and with deep trust in peer reviews.

Buyer behavior

How Canadians choose.

Canadian buyers are deliberate. They research thoroughly, compare carefully, and place enormous weight on reviews — a business with 200 thoughtful reviews beats one with 20, even at a higher price. Trust signals matter more here than urgency tactics; the hard sell backfires.

Bing holds a larger share in Canada than in most countries, so Bing Places and Bing Webmaster deserve real attention, not an afterthought. And the .ca domain still carries trust weight with Canadian searchers — a small signal, but in close races, small signals decide.

The playbook

The Canadian local SEO checklist.

  • Bilingual foundation: English site plus genuine French pages and Business Profile posts for Quebec — written by a human, tuned to Quebec queries.
  • Province-aware location pages: genuinely different pages per market, not find-and-replace clones. Toronto content should not read like Halifax content.
  • Review engine: systematic review generation, because Canadian buyers filter on them harder than most.
  • Bing optimization: Bing Places claimed and optimized, Bing Webmaster Tools monitored — free visibility your competitors ignore.
  • .ca trust signals: consistent NAP with Canadian address formatting, .ca domain where possible, Canadian hosting or CDN.
