Why Spain businesses choose me

Spain rewards businesses that understand Spain. Forty-eight million people, two business capitals in Madrid and Barcelona, strong regional hubs in Valencia, Seville, Bilbao, Malaga and Zaragoza, and a tourism industry that rewires search demand every single year. The businesses winning Spanish search results are not the ones with the biggest budgets. They are the ones whose websites speak to Spanish buyers the way Spanish buyers actually think: in Spanish, with Spanish reviews, Spanish hours, and an understanding that a customer in Barcelona does not behave exactly like a customer in Seville. Language is the first thing outsiders get wrong. Spanish dominates commercial search nationwide, and that will not surprise you. What surprises people is how much the regional languages matter commercially. In Catalonia, a meaningful share of local searches happens in Catalan, and businesses that serve Barcelona in Catalan earn trust that Spanish-only competitors do not. Basque and Galician play the same role in their regions. This is not a political statement; it is search behavior. I research which languages your customers actually type in, then build each language as its own deliberate effort with its own keyword research, never as an afterthought translation. The second thing outsiders get wrong is rhythm. Spain runs on seasons. The coasts, the islands, the cities with festival calendars: demand surges and falls on a timetable, and SEO has a lead time. A restaurant in Marbella that starts thinking about summer rankings in May has already lost. The winners do the work in the quiet months. I plan every tourism-adjacent campaign backwards from the booking window, so content, profiles and reviews are in place before demand arrives. Google is by far the leading search engine here, which keeps the technical playbook focused. Spain is also an early adopter of AI Overviews, appearing in 14 to 16 percent of desktop searches, so content needs to win classic rankings and earn AI citations at the same time. I am a one-person studio: you work directly with me, the audit is free, and the strategy is built around how Spaniards search, compare and buy. Not a translated template. A Spain playbook. A final note on working with me: I am a one-person studio, so the person who audits your site is the person who fixes it. Spanish business owners tell me they are tired of agencies that sell in January and disappear by March. My model is the opposite: direct contact, monthly plain-language reports, and work you can verify yourself in the search results. The free audit is genuinely free, and it will show you exactly where you stand before you spend anything.
