THE PROBLEM

Fifty search markets wearing one flag.

The US is not one market. The way a customer searches in Houston is nothing like the way one searches in Portland, Maine, and neither looks like Miami. One generic SEO strategy cannot cover all three.

I map the keywords the way your city actually searches: your neighborhoods, your suburbs, your competitor gap, and your language mix. Then I build your presence around that map, not around a template that was written for someone else's city.
