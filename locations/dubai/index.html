THE PROBLEM

Dubai doesn't Google like anywhere else.

In most cities, a customer searches, clicks a website, and fills in a form. In Dubai, the journey looks different, and if your business is not visible at every step of it, you lose the customer before you ever get to say hello.

1
Search in English Expats search in English: "salon marina", "best lawyer dubai". EN
2
Compare on Maps Shortlist by star ratings, photos, and reviews. Websites come second. MAPS
3
WhatsApp the business Nobody calls. They message. No WhatsApp button means no enquiry. CHAT
4
Visit, in person Customers drive across the city. Direction requests decide the winner. IRL

The pain: invisible on Maps means invisible in Dubai. A gorgeous website that ranks nowhere on Google Maps, has no reviews, and only exists in English is, to most of the city, not a real business.

The solution: a bilingual presence built the way Dubai actually buys: English and Arabic keywords, a Google Business Profile that looks alive in both languages, reviews that convince expats, and a WhatsApp-first path to contact you.

REVIEWS DRIVE DUBAI

Expats decide on reviews.

Dubai runs on word of mouth, except the word of mouth is Google reviews. An expat choosing between three clinics or salons they have never visited will pick the one with recent, real reviews, usually without ever reading past the star rating.
