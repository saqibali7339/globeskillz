SEO · Dubai

Selling to 200 nationalities.

Dubai’s search market is unlike anywhere else: 90% expat residents from 200+ nationalities, transacting mostly in English, with Arabic as the official language and a luxury-services economy layered over everything. Your customers arrived last year, leave in three, and make decisions fast — which makes search visibility at the moment of need the entire game.

The bilingual reality is the strategic core. English dominates commercial search — expats, tourists and regional business all search in English — but Arabic queries carry high intent in specific categories (government services, local retail, family services) with far less competition. Most Dubai businesses do one language badly; the winners do English excellently and Arabic strategically.

As an SEO expert in Dubai, I build for the transient, high-expectation buyer: complete profiles, review velocity that survives constant churn, English content with Gulf-aware nuance, and Arabic pages where the data — not assumptions — says they pay.

Buyer behavior

How Dubai buys.

Speed and surface. Dubai buyers decide quickly and judge presentation ruthlessly — a dated website or thin profile reads as a dated business. Reviews matter, but recency matters more: with constant population churn, a review from 2022 is ancient history. The businesses winning here run review generation as a permanent operation, not a campaign.

WhatsApp is the conversion channel. Discovery happens on Google; the conversation moves to WhatsApp within minutes. Your SEO has to connect to that reality — click-to-chat prominent, response fast — or the ranking is wasted.

The playbook

Winning Dubai search, step by step.

  1. English excellence firstFlawless English content with Gulf-aware nuance — no generic templates. This is 80%+ of commercial demand.
  2. Strategic ArabicArabic pages for categories where query data shows real demand and thin competition — verified, not assumed.
  3. Review permanenceA review system built for churn: every customer, every time, because last quarter’s reviews are already stale.
  4. WhatsApp conversion pathRankings wired to instant chat: click-to-WhatsApp, fast response, tracked attribution from search to conversation.
