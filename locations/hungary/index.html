Quick answers

Straight answers to what Hungarian business owners ask me before booking a free audit.

Does Hungarian keyword research need a native-level understanding?

Often, yes, and this is where Hungarian SEO differs from most European markets. Hungarian is an agglutinative language: meaning is built by gluing suffixes onto word stems, so one keyword can have dozens of forms. Machine translation produces Hungarian that reads wrong to natives and targets forms nobody types. Keyword research has to be done by someone who understands how Hungarians actually search, including the slang, the abbreviations and the price-comparison phrasing. I build Hungarian keyword maps from real search behavior, not from translated English lists.

Are cookie banners actually enforced in Hungary?

Yes, and the NAIH is watching this area specifically. In a landmark decision it fined a major Hungarian media provider about 25,000 euros for cookie-management failures, the first time it had fined for cookie issues, and signaled a stricter enforcement regime ahead. Your banner must offer a genuine choice, and you are responsible for the third-party modules on your site. Most Hungarian small-business sites run decorative banners that change nothing technically. I fix the banner and the tag firing order together, so your data is both compliant and accurate.

Should I target Budapest or the regional cities first?

Budapest first, almost always. About 1.7 million people, the headquarters, the agencies, the tourism, the highest search volumes: Budapest is where Hungarian search demand concentrates. Debrecen, Szeged, Miskolc, Pecs and Gyor are real regional markets with lighter competition and genuinely local search behavior. The right order is usually Budapest for scale, then the regional cities for efficient wins. My audits map competition per city so we attack where the return is highest first, rather than fighting the hardest battle on day one.
