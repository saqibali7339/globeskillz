Why Croatia businesses stay invisible

Three patterns I keep seeing across Croatian search — and the thinking behind fixing each one.

PROBLEM 01
Croatian-language search is underserved

Most SEO playbooks target English. Croatian queries — cases, diacritics, local phrasing — need research done properly, not translated.

PROBLEM 02
Tourism drowns out local intent

Dubrovnik and Split searches skew tourist. Local businesses need pages that separate visitor intent from resident intent.

PROBLEM 03
Zagreb competition, regional invisibility

The capital takes the broad terms; Rijeka, Osijek and Zadar searches go to whoever targeted them.
