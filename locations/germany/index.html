Germany's privacy sensitivity is cultural, not just legal: the Planet49 case, the TDDDG cookie rules and the DSK's button-parity guidance all grew from how seriously Germans take tracking. Practically, this means your consent banner must offer a real, equally prominent refuse option, and your analytics must respect it. Sites that get this right earn trust; sites with dark patterns get reported. I build consent setups that are compliant and still give you clean data, because in Germany privacy and performance are not opposites.
